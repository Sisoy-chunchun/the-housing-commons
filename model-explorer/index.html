      THE QUESTION BEHIND THE MODELS

      Who is housing
      working for?

      Community ownership changes who controls housing and who benefits from it. Lasting affordability also needs accessible entry costs, sound maintenance, and funding for residents whose incomes cannot cover the cost of housing.

      How to read this guide

      The diagrams simplify representative structures. The examples show how those structures work in specific places. Legal terms, resident rights and protections vary between projects and countries.

      “Operating” means a community or program exists. It does not certify that every affordability goal has been achieved. “Evolving adaptation” marks a structure still being developed. Scenarios explain likely implications of stated rules, not a promise of rent levels, repayment or legal outcomes.

      Capital contributions, ongoing charges, loan interest and resale gains are different things. Removing speculative resale does not remove construction, maintenance or financing costs.
